The Key Players

Click each card to reveal the analogy.

○ 0/6
🛒

The Customer

Your device

💻 Client

Your laptop, phone or console sends requests and waits for answers. Like ordering at a restaurant — you ask, they deliver.

Example: 10.42.3.15
Source Port: Random high (49152–65535)

🏭

The Warehouse

Stores everything

🏢 Server

Powerful computers that store websites, videos and games. Open 24/7, they send data back to any client that asks.

Example: 142.250.204.110
Dest Port: 443 (HTTPS) / 80 (HTTP)

📮

The Post Office

Sorts & forwards

📡 Router

Reads IP addresses on every packet and forwards it toward the right destination. Your gateway to the internet.

Example: 10.42.0.1

🏠

Home Address

Unique to you

📍 IP Address

A unique number for every device on a network. Without it, reply data can never find its way back to you.

Format: 172.16.x.x

📒

The Phone Book

Names → Numbers

📖 DNS

Translates google.com into 142.250.204.110. The internet's phone book.

Example: 8.8.8.8

🧩

Jigsaw Puzzle

Broken into pieces

📦 Packets

Data is split into tiny pieces, each labelled with IPs. They can take different routes, then reassemble in order.

Ports: 443 (HTTPS) / 80 (HTTP)
Each packet has a source IP + dest IP
